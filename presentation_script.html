2 Introduction & Problem Statement

"Good morning/afternoon, esteemed panel members, guests, and colleagues.

Every day, thousands of students, faculty, and workers commute between Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Muntinlupa (PLMun) and commercial transit hubs in Alabang along the Manila South Road corridor. Unfortunately, commuters face severe daily hurdles: unpredictable waiting times at stops, crowded jeepneys with unknown seating availability, and uncertainty regarding road traffic conditions.

At the same time, transport cooperatives and city dispatchers lack real-time visibility into where their fleet is, whether vehicles are bunching together in traffic, or if a driver has suffered a breakdown on the road.

To solve this, we developed JEEPTRACK — an end-to-end, real-time GPS tracking and transit dispatch platform tailored for both modern Euro-4 / Electric and traditional Philippine jeepneys. It connects commuters, drivers, and control center operators in one unified digital ecosystem."

3 System Architecture: The Three Portals

The system is organized into three interconnected, purpose-built interfaces:

  • Commuter Portal: A ride-hailing style responsive web app providing real-time vehicle map locations, arrival countdowns, seat occupancy, and student fare calculation.
  • Driver In-Cab Console: A high-contrast mobile dashboard mounted on the jeepney for route navigation, one-tap passenger counting, and instant Emergency SOS alerting.
  • Central Dispatch Admin Portal: An enterprise operations dashboard for transport cooperative managers and LGU dispatchers to oversee fleet operations, terminal queues, alerts, and analytics.

4 Live Portal Demonstrations

3A. Commuter Portal (Students & Public Transit Riders)

Demo Action: Open the live web link (https://jeepney-tracking.vercel.app) and display the full-screen interactive Leaflet map.
"Let us begin with the Commuter Portal:

• Full-Screen Interactive Map: Inspired by ride-hailing apps like Grab and Move It, commuters immediately see live moving jeepneys along the corridor. Each vehicle icon is rotated dynamically to match the heading direction of the jeepney.
• One-Tap Route Direction: Commuters can seamlessly toggle between PLMun to Alabang and Alabang to PLMun.
• Vehicle Status & Seat Availability: Tapping any approaching jeepney displays its unit code (e.g., PLM-01), live speed, and an exact countdown (e.g., 'Arrival: 4 mins'). Most importantly, it shows available seating — such as '14 / 20 Seats (6 Free)' — eliminating the guesswork of whether an arriving vehicle can accommodate them.
• 2-Minute Proximity Bell Alert: Commuters can activate an arrival bell that chimes when their target vehicle is within 2 minutes of their selected stop.
• Automated 20% Discount Fare Matrix: In strict compliance with LTFRB regulations, the stop selector computes both standard fares and the 20% student/PWD/senior citizen discounted fare automatically."

3B. Driver In-Cab Console (Dashboard Mobile App)

Demo Action: Switch to the Driver view or log in using credentials driver1 / driver123.
"Next is the Driver In-Cab Console, engineered for hands-free clarity while mounted on a jeepney's dashboard:

• Driver Identification & Shift Status: The top bar displays unit PLM-01, driver name, license number, and an active toggle for ON DUTY vs. ON BREAK. Taking a break immediately marks the unit idle so commuters know it is parked at the terminal.
• Live Navigation & Speedometer: Drivers see their speed in km/h, road hazard pins, and a quick-switch route button.
• Thumb-Friendly Passenger Counter: Large '+' and '–' buttons allow drivers to quickly record boarding and alighting passengers without taking their eyes off the road.
• One-Touch Emergency SOS Help: In case of engine failure, flat tire, or medical distress, pressing this button immediately transmits vehicle GPS coordinates and sounds an emergency siren in the central control room.
• Road Problem Reporting: Drivers can report heavy traffic congestion, road works, flash floods, or accidents to broadcast warning pins to fellow drivers."

3C. Central Dispatch Admin Portal (Control Center)

Demo Action: Log in to the Admin Portal using admin / admin123 and navigate through the side navigation tabs.
"Now we arrive at the Central Dispatch Admin Portal, the nerve center for transport operators:

1. Executive KPI Cards: Instant metrics showing Active Units on the road, Daily Passengers serviced, On-Time Dispatch Rate (benchmarked against corridor trip averages), and Active Alerts.
2. Terminal Geofencing: Virtual circular boundaries around PLMun Terminal and Alabang South Station automatically log arrival and departure timestamps without paper clipboards.
3. Operational Alerts Management: Dispatched incidents (Emergency SOS, road deviation, speeding) are listed by priority with acknowledgment and resolution tracking.
4. Passenger Analytics: Visualizes morning (7:00 AM – 9:00 AM) and evening (5:00 PM – 7:00 PM) rush-hour curves to help cooperatives deploy the optimal number of units.
5. Enterprise Datatables: All management tables (Registered Users, Fleet Management, Alerts, Terminals) feature integrated search bars, status filters, CSV export, and pagination."

5 Plain-English Explanations of Complex Terms

Technical Term Plain-English Explanation Real-World Value
GPS Telemetry Continuous stream of vehicle data (latitude, longitude, speed, and compass heading) sent to the server every few seconds. Allows commuters and operators to watch the vehicle move in real time on the road.
Geofencing A virtual boundary (80-meter radius) drawn around terminals and stops. Automatically registers when a jeepney enters or leaves a terminal bay without manual logbooks.
Dynamic ETA Estimated arrival time calculated using actual live vehicle speed and remaining road distance. If traffic slows down near Putatan, arrival time adjusts automatically so waiting riders have accurate info.
Corridor Simulator A virtual driving simulation engine following the exact Manila South Road coordinates. Allows panelists and evaluators to test and demonstrate full tracking features indoors without needing a moving car.
Seat Occupancy Tracking Real-time count of seated passengers versus maximum vehicle capacity (e.g., 20 seats). Stops commuters from waiting for full jeepneys; helps operators optimize passenger flow.

6 Frequently Asked Questions (Defense-Ready Answers)

Q1: "What happens if a driver loses mobile internet connectivity while on the road?"
Answer: "The system automatically preserves the vehicle's last reported location and flags its status as 'GPS Standby' or 'Offline' with an accurate timestamp (e.g., 'Last updated 3 mins ago'). When mobile data reconnects, the telemetry stream resumes immediately. Central dispatch is also alerted if an active vehicle goes silent unexpectedly."
Q2: "Can traditional jeepneys use this system, or is it strictly for Modern PUJs?"
Answer: "JEEPTRACK was built specifically to support both! Traditional PUJs only need an affordable smartphone on a dashboard mount running our web app. Modern Euro-4 and Electric jeepneys can utilize the same app or connect to onboard GPS telemetry units."
Q3: "How does the platform prevent student discount fraud?"
Answer: "The Commuter Portal includes an accredited PLMun Digital Transit ID Pass with a verified student number and photo badge. Commuters present this pass upon boarding to verify their entitlement to the 20% legal discount computed by the fare calculator."
Q4: "Why choose a Progressive Web Application (PWA) over a native app from Google Play or App Store?"
Answer: "A high-performance PWA eliminates the barrier of phone storage space and lengthy app store installation flows. Commuters and drivers can immediately open the link on any smartphone browser while enjoying smooth animations and native touch responsiveness."
